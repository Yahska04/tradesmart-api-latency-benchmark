## 1. The Brand

TradeSmart is a brand that helps you trade smartly in stocks, mutual funds and commodities.

The brand makes investing in the markets accessible to everyone by offering an easy-to-use platform and low brokerage. It combines old-school experience with youthful exuberance and helps and guides customers at every step of their trading journey.

### Brand essence

- Accessible
- Easy to use
- Smart
- Experienced
- Youthful
- Helpful
- Growth-oriented

# 3. Logo Guidelines

## 3.1 Our Logo

The TradeSmart logo depicts a dynamic graph.

The graph represents:

- Growth
- Speed
- Progress
- The dynamic nature of trading

The logo is intended to represent the brand promise of **high growth with speed**.

### Logo construction

The visual concept combines:

```text
Trade Graph
     +
Growth, speed & progress
     =
TradeSmart logo
```

### Do not

- Do not attempt to recreate the logo using the font.
- Do not alter the elements of the logo.
- Do not add special effects to the logo.

---

The TradeSmart brand colours are:

- Yellow
- Red
- Green
- Blue

The brand book associates these colours with stability, wisdom, trust and serenity.

The dark blue colour signifies:

- Strength
- Power

- Do not reproduce the logo in colours other than the specified brand colours.
- Do not alter the background colour of the logo artwork.

# 5. Logo Orientation

There are two permitted logo orientations.

## Standard orientation

The horizontal version should be used predominantly.

## Vertical orientation

The vertical bottom-to-top arrangement is intended exclusively for vertical applications such as:

- Banners
- Streamers
- Signage totems

### Do not

- Do not rotate the logo reading top to bottom.
- Do not rotate the logo at any angle other than the specified orientations.

---

# 6. Logo Exclusive Zone

The logo must have sufficient clear space around it to maintain legibility and distinction.

The clear zone:

- Separates the logo from surrounding elements.
- Protects brand visibility.
- Maximises the logo's visual impact.

### Clear-space rules

No other:

- Text
- Graphic element
- Logo

should intrude into the exclusive zone.

The minimum isolation distance also applies to the distance between the logo and the edge of a document, whether digital or printed.

### Do not

- Do not place elements inside the exclusive zone.
- Do not place the logo directly against the edge of a document.

---

# 7. Logo Scaling

The TradeSmart logo has been designed with a **1:2 ratio**.

The logo may be used at any desired size subject to the specified minimum sizes.

## Minimum size

### Print

**Minimum height/width specification: 25 mm**

### Digital

**Minimum height: 30 pixels**

The minimum size ensures that the subtle details of the logo remain clear.

### Scaling rules

- Always scale proportionately.
- Use vector artwork.
- Do not distort the logo.
- Do not scale the logo disproportionately.
- Do not use a bitmap version when vector artwork is available.

---

## 7.1 Monogram

The monogram version may be used when space is constrained.

Example:

- Favicons
- Small digital placements

### Favicon

The brand book specifies **16 px** as the smallest possible monogram size.

---

# 8. Incorrect Logo Usage

Never:

- Stretch or distort the logo.
- Rotate the logo.
- Reproduce the logo at low resolution.
- Place the logo directly on a background that affects legibility.
- Place the logo on a same-colour or low-contrast background.
- Add shadows.
- Recolour the icon or logo.
- Alter the logo elements.
- Add special effects.
- Recreate the logo using a typeface.

# 12. Brand Language

## 12.1 Brand Tonality

TradeSmart's tone of voice is:

### Smart

Speak in a youthful language.

Present facts and knowledge in an interesting and memorable manner.

**Principle:** Never belabour and never be verbose.

### Knowledgeable

Be the consumer's guide.

- Explain things.
- Hand-hold customers.
- Help customers acquire new knowledge.

### Friendly

Be the consumer's best friend.

- Be honest.
- Speak with affection and respect.

### Unambiguous

Use simple language.

Be direct.

Avoid sentences and phrases that:

- Can be interpreted in multiple ways.
- Require significant effort to understand.

# 18. Copy Style Guide

## 18.1 Headlines

Keep headlines to **8 words or less**.

Headlines should be:

- Simple
- Crisp
- Smart
- Unambiguous

### Headline principle

```text
Short + Clear + Smart + Direct
```

---

## 18.2 Subheads

Use a subhead when needed to lead the headline into the body copy.

Subheads can be longer than headlines, but should still be kept concise.

---

## 18.3 Body Copy

Body copy should:

- Connect naturally with the headline and subhead.
- Flow naturally from sentence to sentence.
- Avoid long sentences.
- Use simple words.
- Use interesting words and phrases.
- Keep the reader's interest alive.

### Copy structure

```text
Headline
    |
    v
Subhead (if required)
    |
    v
Simple, connected body copy
```

# 25. FAQs

## Where do we use CMYK?

CMYK is used for print applications where it is not possible to use Pantone colours.

CMYK represents:

- C = Cyan
- M = Magenta
- Y = Yellow
- K = Black

The brand book notes that offset and most digital printing devices use CMYK as the default colour mode.

Use CMYK where Pantone inks are not possible, including large-format digital printing such as banners and signs.

---

## Why do we need a secondary colour palette?

The secondary colour palette provides a neutral colour option for communication material.

Each secondary colour can be combined individually with TradeSmart Blue.

The secondary palette is limited to communication material for:

- Print
- Web

It should not be used as a substitute for the TradeSmart logo colours.

---

## Do we need the logo as a CDR file?

The brand book does not recommend using CorelDraw because of potential colour-management inaccuracies.

The logo does not need to be provided as a CDR file because Adobe Illustrator vector artwork is compatible with other vector software, including CorelDraw.

---

## When do we use Trebuchet MS?

Trebuchet MS is used predominantly for:

- Web
- Digital applications
- PowerPoint presentations

It is a default system font on PCs and Macs.

For other applications, use the Roboto family.
